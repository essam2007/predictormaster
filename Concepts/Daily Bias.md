# Daily Bias

**Which direction to hunt** — the HTF lean that every intraday setup must agree
with. One of the five rubric pillars.

## How it's formed (this strategy)
- **[[True Opens]]:** price above the Daily True Open (midnight) ⇒ bullish lean; below ⇒ bearish.
- **HTF delivery (1h/4h):** are we delivering up or down on the higher timeframes?
- **Draw on liquidity:** which pool ([[Liquidity & LRLR|BSL/SSL]]) is price most likely drawn toward next?
- **Quarterly phase:** where are we in [[The Four Quarters (AMD-X)|AMD-X]] on the daily/weekly?

A setup that fights daily bias gets graded down; full alignment (bias + HTF
delivery + killzone + SSMT + PSP + FVG/IFVG) is the A+ stack.
