# Precision Swing Point (PSP)

The **lower-timeframe confirmation** that institutions have stepped in at the
turn. Pairs with [[Sequential SMT (SSMT)]]: SSMT says *where/which way*, PSP says
*now*.

## Definition
A PSP is a **divergence in candle closes between correlated assets** at a turning
point — at the same candle, one asset closes **bullish** while a correlated asset
closes **bearish**.

- **Bullish PSP:** at a low, one asset's (middle) candle closes bearish while the
  correlated asset closes bullish → downtrend weakness → reversal up.
- **Bearish PSP:** at a high, one closes bullish while the other closes bearish →
  uptrend weakness → reversal down.

Typically read on the **5–15 minute** timeframe to confirm a Market Structure
Break (MSB) flagged by SSMT.

## Role in the stack
SSMT (bias) → **PSP (trigger/confirmation)** → [[Fair Value Gap (FVG)]] /
[[Inverse FVG (IFVG) — DodgysDD Model]] (entry). A two-stage PSP+SMT combo is a
common high-conviction filter.
