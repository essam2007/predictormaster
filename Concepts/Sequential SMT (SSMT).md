# Sequential SMT (SSMT)

[[SMT Divergence]] measured **across quarters** — the Quarterly-Theory version of
the correlation tell, and a top-weighted pillar of this strategy.

## Definition
Two+ correlated assets (ES/NQ/YM) both print a similar swing (high/low) in a
**previous quarter** (say Q3). In the **current quarter** (Q4), **only one** asset
breaks that swing level — the other **fails to reach it**. That failure-to-confirm
*between quarters* is the Sequential SMT.

- Gives **directional bias**: the asset that *didn't* follow reveals the real lean.
- Can be read between **sessions, days, or weeks** (fractal).

## Two-stage / nested SSMT
The strongest version is a **two-stage** divergence: a stage-1 SSMT sets the lean,
and a nested stage-2 SSMT inside the killzone confirms the precise turn. (This is
the "true nested two-stage SMT" the deck flags as a roadmap upgrade.)

## Confirmation
SSMT is a *bias/timing* tool — it is confirmed for entry by a
[[Precision Swing Point (PSP)]] on the 5–15m, then executed via the
[[FVG + IFVG Combo & V-Shape]].
