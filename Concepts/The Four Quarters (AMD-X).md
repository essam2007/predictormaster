# The Four Quarters (AMD-X)

How a single cycle unfolds. Two orderings exist; the dominant one is **AMD-X**.

## AMD-X (the default)
| Quarter | Phase | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | **A**ccumulation | Consolidation; range builds, the "groundwork" |
| **Q2** | **M**anipulation | **Judas swing** — false move / stop-hunt against the real direction. Starts at the [[True Opens\|True Open]] |
| **Q3** | **D**istribution | The real expansion; **low-resistance liquidity run** toward target |
| **Q4** | **X** (Continuation/Reversal) | Continues or reverses the prior quarter |

## X-AMD (the variant)
Sometimes Q1 is already expansion (X), Q2 accumulates, Q3 manipulates, Q4
distributes. Knowing which variant you're in is part of reading [[Daily Bias]].

## The trade logic
You want to be **buying/selling the Q2 manipulation** (the Judas swing that traps
retail) and **riding Q3 distribution** into [[Liquidity & LRLR|liquidity]]. The
manipulation leg is where [[Sequential SMT (SSMT)]] and [[Precision Swing Point (PSP)]]
flag the turn, and where the [[FVG + IFVG Combo & V-Shape]] entry fires.
