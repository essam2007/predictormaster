# SMT Divergence

**Smart Money Technique / Tool.** A divergence between **correlated assets** —
the core "tell" that a move is a trap rather than the real direction.

## Definition
When one asset makes a **new high/low** but a correlated asset **fails** to
confirm (doesn't make the matching high/low), the break is suspect → likely a
[[The Four Quarters (AMD-X)|manipulation]] sweep, not real expansion.

- **Bearish SMT:** asset A makes a higher high, asset B makes a lower high → sell-side tell
- **Bullish SMT:** asset A makes a lower low, asset B makes a higher low → buy-side tell

## The index triad (this strategy)
**ES / NQ / YM** are the correlated set. A sweep of a level by one index that the
others **don't** confirm is the reversal signal.

- Confirmed on the lower timeframe by a [[Precision Swing Point (PSP)]]
