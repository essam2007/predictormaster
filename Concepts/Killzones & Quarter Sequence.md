# Killzones & Quarter Sequence

**When** to hunt. Killzones are the high-volatility windows where ICT setups have
the best odds; the **quarter sequence** is how Quarterly Theory pinpoints the
exact 90-minute slice inside a killzone where manipulation→distribution fires.

## The session quarters (NY day)
Each 6-hour session splits into four 90-minute quarters. The **NY AM** session is
the primary hunting ground for index reversals:
- Look for **Q2 manipulation** (the Judas swing) to set the trap
- Trade the **Q3 distribution** leg toward [[Liquidity & LRLR|liquidity]]

## Practical anchors used in the deck
- **NY AM killzone**, **09:50–10:10 macro**, **Silver Bullet** window
- **08:30 news block** (avoid), **Tue/Wed preferred**, **Fri avoid**
