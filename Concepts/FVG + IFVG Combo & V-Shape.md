# FVG + IFVG Combo & V-Shape

The **reversal entry pattern** you flag on your calendar — the DodgysDD-style
combo that produces a sharp **V-shaped** turn.

## The pattern (as commonly taught)
1. **Liquidity sweep** of a prior high/low → stop-hunt ([[The Four Quarters (AMD-X)|Q2 manipulation]]).
2. **[[SMT Divergence]] / [[Sequential SMT (SSMT)]]** at the extreme — correlated index doesn't confirm.
3. **Displacement back through the [[Fair Value Gap (FVG)]]**, inverting it → [[Inverse FVG (IFVG) — DodgysDD Model]].
4. **Entry on the IFVG retest**; stop beyond the sweep wick; target opposing
   [[Liquidity & LRLR|liquidity]]. The price path looks like a **sharp V** (down-then-up
   or up-then-down) rather than a rounded base.

## Why "combo"
The edge is the **stack**: sweep + SMT + FVG→IFVG inversion happening together at
a [[True Opens|True Open]] / inside a [[Killzones & Quarter Sequence|killzone]].
Each missing piece steps the grade down (see [[Strategy ↔ Code Map]] + the rubric
in the trader repo).
