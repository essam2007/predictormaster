# ICT Trading Journal — Obsidian vault

A markdown vault for marking up setups to your grading rubric and turning your discretionary
edge into a labeled training dataset for the deck. Open in **Obsidian** (free at obsidian.md →
*Open folder as vault* → point it at this `journal/` folder), or read on GitHub.

## Layout
- `Strategy/Grading-Rubric.md` — the A+ → D ladder (the IP). **Has 5 open questions for you.**
- `Strategy/Glossary.md` — my definitions of SSMT/PSP/FVG/LRLR… (correct where wrong).
- `Strategy/Morning-Routine.md` — your pre-session process, mapped to engine signals.
- `Templates/Setup-Template.md` — the per-setup markup format (front-matter tags feed the dataset).
- `Setups/` — **5 worked examples**, one per grade, for you to critique:
  - `01-Aplus-NQ-long` — all five pillars (A+).
  - `02-A-NQ-long-no-liquidity` — no clean drawn liquidity/LRLR (A).
  - `03-Aminus-NQ-short-one-stage` — one-stage SSMT only (A−).
  - `04-Bplus-NQ-long-no-htf` — no HTF delivery (B+).
  - `05-Bminus-NQ-short-friday-lowconfluence` — multiple misses, likely a pass (B−).
- `Training-Plan.md` — how we go from intuition → calibrated engine.

> ⚠️ The 5 setups are **ILLUSTRATIVE** — constructed to test whether I've understood your rubric
> and process, since I can't pull live charts from here. Once your TradingView bar-feed streams
> into the deck (or you share chart exports), I mark up **real** ones the same way.

## How to critique (this is the training signal)
In each setup file, fill **§6 "Your verdict"** (take/pass + why) and fix my **grade** if wrong.
Edit `Grading-Rubric.md`'s open questions. Reply in chat with the highlights and I produce the
next, sharper batch — the `/loop` advances on your critique, not a timer.
